NEAR EAST UNIVERSITY - UNDERGRADUATE ACADEMIC REGULATIONS KNOWLEDGE BASE

Article 14 - Course Registration and Add-Drop Rules:
Students must complete their course registration at the beginning of each semester within the dates specified in the Academic Calendar.
During the official Add-Drop week (the second week of classes), students may add new courses or drop existing courses with the approval of their academic advisor.
No tuition penalty or transcript grade ("W") is recorded for courses dropped during the official Add-Drop period.
First-year students cannot drop compulsory first-semester courses without special faculty board permission.

Article 15 - Course Withdrawal ("W" Grade) Rules:
After the Add-Drop period ends, students may withdraw from a course until the "Last Day for Course Withdrawal" (18 December 2026 for the Fall 2026 semester).
A withdrawn course appears with a "W" grade on the transcript and is not included in the GPA/CGPA calculation.
A student may withdraw from at most two (2) courses per semester and at most six (6) courses throughout their entire undergraduate program.

Article 18 - Attendance Requirements:
Students are required to attend at least 70% of theoretical lectures and at least 80% of laboratory/practical sessions.
Students who fail to meet the attendance requirement receive an automatic "NA" (Non-Attendance) grade and are not permitted to take the Final examination for that course.

Article 22 - Examinations and Assessment (Midterm & Final Exams):
Every course includes at least one Midterm examination and one Final examination.
Fall 2026 Midterm Examinations are held between 07 November 2026 and 14 November 2026.
A student who misses a Midterm examination due to a documented medical report approved by the Faculty Administrative Board may take a Make-up examination.
There is no make-up examination for the Final exam; students who miss or fail the Final exam take the Resit examination.

Article 25 - Grading Scale and Academic Standing (GPA / CGPA):
Passing letter grades are AA (4.00), BA (3.50), BB (3.00), CB (2.50), CC (2.00).
Conditional passing grades are DC (1.50) and DD (1.00), which are considered successful if the student's Cumulative GPA (CGPA) is at least 2.00.
Failing grades are FF (0.00) and NA (0.00). To graduate, a student must complete all curriculum courses and achieve a minimum CGPA of 2.00 out of 4.00.
